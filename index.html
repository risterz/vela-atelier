About Us:

At VELA, our fascination with design has never been limited to permanent objects.

With roots in architecture and culinary craft, we have always been drawn to the tension between geometry, fermentation, glaze reflection, and warmth. Over time, that sensibility extended beyond galleries and into ephemeral patisserie.

Inspired by modernism and pure single-origin ingredients, we explore how elementary form, patient 48-hour dough proofing, and luminous glazes can transform a familiar donut into a quiet daily ceremony.

For us, VELA is another way to express what we have always loved about form. Only this time, it is meant to be shared warm.

A LOVE FOR FORM, IN EVERY CRUMB.
